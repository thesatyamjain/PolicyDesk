Core capabilities

Built for the vault.
Not the dashboard.

Most policy trackers live in the cloud and sell you reminders as a feature. PolicyDesk treats your data as your property — all records stay on the device that belongs to you.

01

Local-First Architecture

All vehicle records, customer data, and policy documents are encrypted in a local Room SQLite database and secured using the Android Keystore. Nothing leaves your device without your direct action.

02

Proactive Expiry Alerts

WorkManager-scheduled notifications fire at 30, 15, 7, and 1 day before expiry — and on expiry day itself. Scheduled for 9 AM daily, auto-rescheduled after device reboot.

03

AI Document Extraction

Paste or share a policy PDF and the app extracts all key fields automatically. Powered by Cloudflare Workers AI with optional Gemini, OpenAI, or Claude using your own API key.

Google Drive Vault

Your vault.
Only your files.

PolicyDesk requests one narrow OAuth scope: drive.file. This gives the app access to only the folder it created — the PolicyDesk Vault. It cannot read, modify, or see any other file on your Google Drive, ever.
